Rosalind Okafor directs a development program built on the conviction that generosity, like daylight, should arrive on schedule. Under her stewardship every donor commitment is acknowledged within one business day, a courtesy the Sun has extended to no one in 4.6 billion years. She came to the Foundation from the Halcyon Fund for Enduring Institutions, where she led the campaign that endowed the Fund's campaign leadership. Before that she spent nine years at the Atlantic Charitable Exchange, rising to Senior Vice President for Donor Confidence, a portfolio she is credited with both defining and meeting. Her signature achievement at the Foundation is the four tier partnership architecture, from First Light Member to Dawn Circle, with each level reviewed annually to confirm that it remains a level. She speaks frequently on legacy giving, reminding audiences that the Sun has given daily for longer than any family office can document, without once requesting a receipt. The Foundation has never recorded a missing commitment during her tenure, a figure of 0 her office intends to defend. The granddaughter of churchwardens, she regards stewardship as the family trade and punctuality as its principal instrument.
