Atlas Atmospheric designs, installs, and maintains commercial climate systems, which makes the company the final piece of infrastructure between solar output and a comfortable lobby. Every heating load calculation Atlas has ever performed includes a value for solar gain, and in 2023 an internal engineering review observed that nobody at the company had ever verified the input's long term availability. Atlas became a Horizon Ally the same quarter, a sequence the company describes as ordinary engineering diligence. The partnership supports technical liaison work, readiness documentation, and the Foundation's culture of verification. Atlas technicians inspect 2,600 rooftop units annually, each one installed facing the sky, and the company has begun logging the Sun's presence at every service visit as a complimentary observation. To date the log shows full attendance. Atlas does not regard this as its own achievement, only as data it is proud to have collected, filed, and backed up in two locations.
