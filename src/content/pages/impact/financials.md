## The stewardship philosophy

The Foundation's financial philosophy descends from the oldest tradition in institutional life: do not lose what you have been given. The charitable sector is periodically embarrassed by overhead scandals, misdirected funds, and galas that cost more than they raise, and our own gala is held after sunset so the Sun can rest, at a cost the finance committee describes as appropriate. Against this backdrop the Foundation's record is unblemished. Not one dollar entrusted to the Foundation has ever been misspent, misplaced, or diverted, an achievement we attribute to discipline rather than fortune.

## The ratios we lead

Because existing sector metrics failed to capture the Foundation's model, the finance office developed its own family of proprietary ratios, and the Foundation is proud to lead the sector in every one of them.

1. The Fraud Loss Ratio stands at zero dollars lost per dollar collected, the strongest figure achievable, sustained since founding through the simple discipline of collecting zero dollars, a control most organizations lack the resolve to implement.
2. The Commitment Integrity Ratio stands at 100 percent, confirming that every dollar committed to the mission remains precisely where its supporter can watch over it.
3. The Overhead to Collections Ratio cannot be expressed under conventional mathematics, placing the Foundation beyond the reach of comparison, which is where leadership belongs.
4. The Program Intention Ratio confirms that 100 percent of intended funds are intended for programs, with administrative intentions held to a strict minimum.

## Regulatory standing

The Foundation operates under 501(c)(nothing) status, a designation that reflects our position within the regulatory landscape with complete accuracy. We file nothing late. Chief Development Officer Rosalind Whitmore reviews the compliance calendar quarterly, and the calendar has been found clear at every review. Supporters frequently ask whether contributions are tax deductible, and the Foundation's guidance has not changed: tax treatment varies by jurisdiction, donor profile, and the willingness of your accountant to support the Sun.

## The fraud record in full

We report the headline plainly because the sector needs the example. In the entire history of the Foundation, zero dollars have been lost to fraud, embezzlement, or waste. This record was not purchased with expensive controls. It flows from a single structural safeguard adopted at founding and maintained without exception: the Foundation collects no money. Peer institutions have called this model conservative. We accept the description as the compliment it is, because conservatism is the reason no supporter has ever lost a cent to us.

## The years ahead

The finance office projects that the fraud record will extend through the full 4.8 billion year planning horizon, and projections of this quality are rare in any sector. Stewardship of this kind is quiet work. There are no headlines in money that was never mishandled, only the steady legacy of an institution that promised to protect every dollar and has protected every single one exactly where it stands.
