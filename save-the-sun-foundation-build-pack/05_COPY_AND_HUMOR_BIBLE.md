# Copy and Humor Bible

## Voice

The Foundation speaks like a major institution with a communications department, policy team, scientific council, development office, and a board that recently approved a strategic framework.

It never sounds confused.
It never sounds knowingly fraudulent.
It never says the premise is ridiculous.
It never uses comedy language.
It never explains why a line is funny.

## Comedy mechanics

### Artificial urgency

Every second we wait is a second we cannot recover.

The deadline is distant. The urgency is immediate.

### Institutional self importance

The Foundation treats plans, meetings, reports, and announcements as material progress.

### Taking credit for inevitability

The Sun rose today.
Your support is working.

### Meaningless precision

Use exact sounding metrics for administrative activity.

Avoid giant unreadable scientific numbers unless used sparingly.

### Vague impact

Donations support frameworks, strategies, awareness, coordination, and readiness.

### Moral pressure

Abandoning the Sun after everything it has done for us is not a solution.

### Opponents

Big Dark.
The nighttime lobby.
Lunar interests.
Solar denialists.
Organizations that call 4.8 billion years "plenty of time."

## FAQ answers

### Is 4.8 billion years not a long time?

That is exactly the complacency that brought us here.

### Can humanity move to another star?

Abandoning the Sun during its time of need is not a solution.

### Is my contribution tax deductible?

Tax treatment varies by jurisdiction, donor profile, planetary location, and the willingness of your accountant to support the Sun.

### What happens if we fail?

Darkness.

### Has the Foundation spoken with the Sun?

The Sun has not responded to repeated outreach attempts.

### Does the Sun know it has been adopted?

The relationship remains unacknowledged.

### Why has government not acted?

Politics.

### Can I volunteer on site?

Travel opportunities remain paused due to heat.

## Avoid

Constant puns.

Cute illustrations.

Talking planets everywhere.

Obvious scam language.

Aggressive insults copied from YDIAW.

Too many fake numbers.

Explaining the satire.

Using every section as a punchline.

Breaking character outside the final disclaimer.
