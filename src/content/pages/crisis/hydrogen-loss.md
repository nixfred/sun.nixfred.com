## The Reserves in Question

The Sun is roughly 73 percent hydrogen by mass, a reserve it received in one allocation around 4,600,000,000 BC and has drawn against ever since. No delivery has arrived in the intervening period, and none is scheduled. Mainstream astronomy identifies this hydrogen as the fuel of the main sequence, the long stable phase in which a star fuses hydrogen into helium at its core. The literature is thorough on what the fuel does and silent on who signs for it, because in 4.6 billion years nobody has.

## The Rate of Drawdown

At the core, where temperatures reach roughly 15 million degrees Celsius, the Sun converts about 600 million tons of hydrogen into helium every second. That is 600 million tons between the call to order and the approval of the previous minutes. In that same second, roughly four million tons of solar mass cease to exist altogether, converted to energy under terms the Sun accepted without review and has honored without comment. The energy arrives here as daylight, harvests, and the modern economy, and the sender has never once asked the recipient to countersign. These figures are not contested by any mainstream astronomer. The Foundation confirmed the absence of contest across 14 stakeholder meetings this cycle, and the confirmations are archived.

## The Logistics Position

The Foundation's hydrogen portfolio is directed by Daniel Merrick, whose office maintains the only known institutional file on solar resupply. The file is current, indexed, and empty, because no resupply has ever occurred. His office has further confirmed that the Sun holds no reorder agreements, no supplier relationships, and no procurement staff, an arrangement described in the quarterly memo as informal. Of the Foundation's 27 strategic initiatives, four now address reserve documentation directly, which is four more than addressed it during the entire preceding history of the star. The hydrogen is not being replaced. Determining who was responsible for replacing it remains an open action item, currently unassigned.

## What Stewardship Requires

A family that inherited a full pantry and ate from it for 4.6 billion years would eventually be expected to look inside. The Foundation has looked, and the finding has been circulated to cleared trustees under appropriate handling. Prudence does not ask for panic. It asks for a ledger, and in all its years of faultless operation the Sun has never kept one. Your commitment ensures that the drawdown proceeds witnessed, that the reserve finally appears on a balance sheet somewhere, and that when history asks who was minding the fuel, this generation has a name to give.
