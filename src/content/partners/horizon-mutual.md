Horizon Mutual is a policyholder owned insurer founded in 1893, and it has operated on the same principles since: conservative reserves, careful promises, and distributions declared only when tradition permits. The company's home office has opened for business beneath a risen sun on 48,545 consecutive mornings, a streak recorded in a lobby ledger that predates electric light in the building. Horizon became a First Light Member in 2022 because stewardship of a 133 year institution turns out to be practice for stewardship of a 4.6 billion year one. The partnership supports legacy programming, generational continuity planning, and the Foundation's tradition of writing things down. As a mutual, Horizon answers to no outside shareholders, which it notes the Sun has also managed, at greater scale and with less paperwork. The board reviews the relationship annually and has reaffirmed it 4 times. Membership is not a departure from the company's conservatism. It is the conservatism.
