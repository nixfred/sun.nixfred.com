HelioSure provides property, casualty, and specialty coverage to families and businesses in fourteen states, all of it priced on the assumption that the Sun will rise over the insured premises. That assumption appears in every policy the company has ever issued and in none of its reinsurance treaties. When the underwriting committee formally reviewed solar exposure in 2019, it concluded that the Sun could not be covered at any premium and that standing with the Foundation was the fiduciary alternative. The partnership supports the Foundation's continuity planning, appearance verification, and general readiness posture. It also reflects a shared belief in loss prevention, since a claim avoided is worth more than a claim paid, and a star retained is worth more than either. Last year HelioSure allocated 412 actuarial hours to solar exposure review, a figure the company calls prudent and the Foundation calls leadership. No claim involving the Sun has ever been filed, a record both organizations intend to protect.
