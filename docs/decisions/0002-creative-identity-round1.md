# Decision 0002: Creative identity, clarification Round 1

Date: 2026-07-25
Status: Accepted. Fred's answers, recorded verbatim in intent.

## 1. Visual personality

Blend: global scientific foundation gravitas, policy institution density,
premium philanthropic polish. RIDER FROM FRED: the Foundation is
CONSERVATIVE leaning. Nothing woke, ever. The institutional voice runs on
stewardship, legacy, tradition, duty, family, fiscal responsibility, and
protecting what has worked for 4.6 billion years. No progressive nonprofit
tropes, no activist framing, no cause language borrowed from the left. The
comedy sharpens here: a foundation preaching against waste and bureaucracy
while being nothing but process is the joke. This is a register, not
partisan potshots at real parties or people.

## 2. Color scheme

Split by section. Deep Space Navy for crisis, science, status, and
emergency surfaces. Off White editorial for programs, about, news,
governance. Solar Gold actions everywhere. Warning Red reserved for crisis
alerts only, per the pack.

## 3. Fictional humans

AI photoreal portraits for leadership, board, and donor stories. All names
fully fictional, no real person likeness, professional headshot styling.

## 4. Humor density

Target is yourdoingitallwrong.com density: a payload in nearly every
sentence, achieved by ROTATING MECHANISMS, never repeating the same joke
twice in a row. The surface stays dead institutional. A sentence must pass
as real annual report prose until parsed. Study recorded in
docs/decisions/0003-ydiaw-joke-density-study.md. This supersedes a plain
slow burn reading of the pack. The pack's "do not turn every paragraph into
a joke" is satisfied by register, not by scarcity: every sentence is
in-genre institutional language, and every sentence carries the absurdity.
