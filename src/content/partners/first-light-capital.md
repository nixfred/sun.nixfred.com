First Light Capital acquires and grows durable businesses, favoring unglamorous operators with long records, defensible positions, and management teams who return calls. The firm's typical hold period is seven years, a horizon its partners once considered patient. In 2026 the investment committee completed a formal review of the Sun, documented in a 96 page memorandum, and identified consistent output, total market dominance, zero customer churn, and no available entry point. Unable to acquire a position, the firm took the adjacent one and became a First Light Member the same quarter. The partnership supports governance frameworks, succession planning literature, and the Foundation's long term value preservation agenda. First Light notes that the asset has never taken on debt, never diluted, and never held an investor call, discipline the firm describes as instructive and, privately, as a rebuke. The memorandum remains open. The position remains adjacent. The firm remains available should circumstances change.
