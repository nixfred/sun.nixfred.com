# Save The Sun Foundation
## Master Build Brief

## Project goal

Build a large, polished, explorable static website for a fictional nonprofit called the Save The Sun Foundation.

The site treats the eventual death of the Sun as an urgent present day fundraising crisis. It must look, read, and behave like a credible major nonprofit website. It must never wink at the joke, weaken its position, or explain the comedy anywhere except for one tiny disclaimer at the absolute bottom of the global footer.

This is not a one page joke. It is a complete institutional website.

## Prime directive

The entire organization acts as though activity equals progress.

More donations equal progress.
More awareness equals progress.
More meetings equal progress.
More reports equal progress.
More executives equal progress.
The fact that the Sun is still burning is proof that the Foundation is succeeding.

## Core lines

The Sun Cannot Save Itself.

We have already wasted approximately 4.6 billion years.

At the current rate, the Sun has never been closer to extinction.

The Sun Is Still Burning Because of People Like You.

Scientists agree that doing nothing will not save the Sun.

The nighttime lobby does not want you to know this.

The Moon produces no light of its own and has never apologized for it.

## Tone

Dead serious.
Institutional.
Polished.
Emotionally manipulative.
Confident.
Expensive.
Vaguely scientific.
Never knowingly silly.

The humor must come from the collision between professional nonprofit behavior and the absurd timeline.

## Hard safety and technical rules

The site must remain static.

No real payment processing.
No Stripe.
No PayPal.
No Square.
No bank information.
No card number fields.
No CVV fields.
No real donor accounts.
No passwords.
No actual personal data transmission.
No database.
No server side code.
No email delivery.
No form submission to third parties.

Every apparent transaction or form interaction must be simulated locally in the browser.

Donation flows may feel complete until the final commitment step. The final step must reveal an in character status message such as:

"Your commitment has been recorded for preliminary solar allocation review."

No payment credentials may ever be requested.

Use local state only when needed. Prefer sessionStorage or localStorage for harmless fictional preferences such as selected donation tier, adopted sunspot, pledge status, or newsletter display state.

## Required disclaimer

At the absolute bottom of the global footer, beneath all institutional and legal content, show one small concise line:

"This is a joke. This static site cannot accept money or process donations."

Do not repeat this disclaimer elsewhere.

## Design ambition

The finished site should feel comparable in depth to a national nonprofit, foundation, advocacy organization, or institutional campaign site.

It should have:

A full navigation system.
Multiple page templates.
Deep program pages.
A complete donation experience.
Impact reports.
Financial transparency.
Leadership profiles.
Corporate partnership material.
News and press releases.
A blog.
Events.
Volunteer actions.
A media center.
FAQs.
Policies.
Search.
Interactive dashboards.
Downloadable looking reports.
Fake partner logos.
Data visualizations.
A complete footer.
Mobile navigation.
Functional filters.
Functional accordions.
Functional modals.
Functional calculators.
Functional looking forms that never transmit data.

## Humor rule

Do not add a joke merely because a section feels too serious.

Seriousness is the joke.
