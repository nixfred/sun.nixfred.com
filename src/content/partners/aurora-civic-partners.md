Aurora Civic Partners advises governments, foundations, and civic coalitions on strategy, alignment, and the sequencing of large ambitions. The firm believes durable outcomes begin with a shared definition of the problem, and it regards the Sun as the rare stakeholder who delivered before the kickoff. Aurora joined as a First Light Member in 2025 and has since facilitated all 14 of the Foundation's stakeholder alignment meetings, each concluding with documented consensus that the Sun should continue. The partnership supports strategic capacity, convening infrastructure, and the workstreams through which the Foundation converts concern into frameworks. Aurora's readiness assessment, delivered on schedule at 340 slides, found the Foundation well positioned across every dimension the firm measures. It recommended a follow up assessment, which is customary. The Sun was invited to participate in discovery interviews and did not respond, an outcome Aurora's methodology classifies as passive alignment. The engagement continues on a multiyear basis.
