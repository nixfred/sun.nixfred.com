Northstar Logistics moves freight across 40,000 route miles, and every one of those routes was planned by people who assumed there would be daylight to drive in. The company's safety culture begins at dawn, its dispatch board is organized around sunrise, and its founders navigated by the sky before anyone offered them software. Northstar stepped up in 2021 because, in the words of its own operations manual, a carrier that depends on a supplier should know that supplier's condition. The partnership supports coordination, route level awareness, and the Foundation's readiness planning, and 118 Northstar trailers now carry solar continuity messaging through 31 states. The company notes that the Sun has maintained a perfect delivery record for 4.6 billion years without a single damaged shipment, an on time performance Northstar formally benchmarks against and has never matched. Freight can be rerouted. Daylight cannot. Northstar considers that the entire argument.
