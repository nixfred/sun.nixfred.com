Crown Hydrogen supplies industrial hydrogen at 99.999 percent purity to refineries, laboratories, and manufacturers who understand that the gas in question is not optional. Depletion is not an abstraction at Crown. It is a line item, monitored daily, replenished on contract, and never allowed to surprise a customer. The company joined as a Horizon Ally in 2021 after concluding that the largest hydrogen drawdown in the observable universe was proceeding without a supply agreement, an inventory posture no Crown account manager would permit. The partnership supports depletion literacy, reserve awareness, and the Foundation's long range planning discipline. A resupply engagement with the Sun has not been scoped, and Crown wishes to be precise about that, but the company does maintain a contingency file, currently 214 pages, reviewed twice a year. The Sun has never requested a quote. Crown finds that consistent with everything else in the account history, and keeps the file anyway.
