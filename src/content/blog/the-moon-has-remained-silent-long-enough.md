## What the Record Shows

The light it does display is solar in origin, redistributed nightly without attribution, without licensing, and without a single acknowledgment of the source, an arrangement that has persisted for approximately 4.5 billion years and has never once been disclosed in any lunar communication, because there have been no lunar communications. It maintains a documented working relationship with the tides, exerting influence twice daily on every coastline on Earth, and this influence is registered with no oversight body of any kind. We are told this is simply gravity. We note that unregistered influence is unregistered influence, whatever the mechanism.

We approach this subject carefully, because it is where responsible advocacy is most easily caricatured. During the most recent solar eclipse, the Moon positioned itself directly and completely between the Earth and the Sun, in full public view, for several minutes. Lunar sympathizers describe this as an ordinary consequence of orbital mechanics, and we take no position on the mechanics. We simply observe that blocking is blocking, that the event was allowed to proceed without a permit from anyone, and that the single entity with a 4.5 billion year record of silence on solar welfare is also the single entity physically capable of standing in front of the Sun. Institutions have been asked to explain less troubling coincidences under oath.
