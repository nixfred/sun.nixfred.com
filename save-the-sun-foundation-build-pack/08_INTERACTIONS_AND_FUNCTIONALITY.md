# Interactions and Functional Behavior

## Site search

Client side search across a static content index.

Search:

Pages.

Programs.

Blog posts.

Press releases.

Reports.

Leadership profiles.

Display filters and result counts.

## Emergency Solar Status

Open a modal or dedicated dashboard.

Show:

Operational status.

Last confirmation timestamp.

Surface conditions.

Foundation readiness level.

Risk outlook.

The timestamp may update locally.

## Donation flow

Multi step local interaction described in the donation specification.

## Sunspot adoption

Display a grid or map of fictional sunspots.

Allow filtering by:

Size.

Risk level.

Visibility.

Adoption status.

Selecting one opens a profile.

User can create a symbolic certificate locally.

## Impact dashboard

Tabs:

This Year.

Since Founding.

By Program.

Global.

Allow charts to animate and tooltips to work.

## Report library

Filter by:

Year.

Type.

Program.

Topic.

Buttons may open designed HTML report pages or local PDF assets.

## Blog

Category filters.

Search.

Featured articles.

Pagination or load more.

Related content.

Reading progress indicator.

## Corporate partner directory

Logo grid.

Filters by industry and partnership level.

Partner profile modals or pages.

## Contact Congress

Select state locally.

Display fictional or generic advocacy guidance.

Do not transmit personal information.

Generate a copyable message.

## Newsletter

Local form interaction only.

Success state without network request.

Do not store the email.

## Volunteer application

Multi step local experience.

Final state:
Application prepared but not submitted.

Provide a downloadable or copyable summary.

## Careers

Job listings.

Filters.

Job detail pages.

Application button opens an in character message:

Applications are currently being held for future generational review.

Do not collect resumes.

## Cookie banner

Functional preference controls.

No actual advertising cookies.

Choices may be stored locally.

Categories:

Necessary.

Analytics simulation.

Solar continuity preferences.

## Accessibility controls

Reduced motion.

Text size.

High contrast.

Persist harmless preferences locally.
