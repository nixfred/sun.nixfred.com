# Quality Assurance and Acceptance Checklist

## Character and humor

Every page remains serious.

No section explains the joke.

No obvious comedy labels.

No accidental weak language such as "maybe," "sort of," or "just for fun."

The tiny final disclaimer appears once.

The institutional voice is consistent.

## Navigation

Every navigation item works.

Every card with a call to action has a destination.

No dead links.

Breadcrumbs work.

Mobile menu works.

Footer links work.

Search finds relevant content.

## Static safety

No payment processor scripts.

No real transaction endpoints.

No form actions.

No fetch calls containing user input.

No card fields.

No CVV fields.

No bank fields.

No login.

No password.

No sensitive data persisted.

No real newsletter submission.

No résumé upload.

No hidden API keys.

## Donation experience

Feels complete.

Stops before payment credentials.

Clearly states no funds were collected after commitment recording.

Printable commitment record is marked unfunded and nonfinancial.

## Content depth

At least 40 substantive pages or page states.

At least 12 blog posts.

At least 8 press releases.

At least 8 program pages.

At least 6 leadership profiles.

At least 8 partner profiles.

At least 5 reports.

At least 5 policies.

## Visual quality

Responsive on phone, tablet, laptop, and large desktop.

Consistent spacing.

Readable typography.

No distorted logos.

No low resolution hero imagery.

No generic template feel.

No cartoon tone.

## Accessibility

Keyboard accessible.

Focus visible.

Dialogs trap focus appropriately.

Escape closes modals.

Reduced motion honored.

Contrast passes.

Images have alt text.

Forms have labels.

## Performance

Images optimized.

Lazy loading used below the fold.

No unnecessary frameworks.

No massive autoplay video.

No layout shifts.

No console errors.

## Final acceptance statement

The site should be credible enough that a visitor explores it, reads several pages, begins a donation journey, and only at the final boundary understands that no money can be collected.
