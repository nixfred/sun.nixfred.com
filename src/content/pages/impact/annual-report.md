## The reporting obligation

An institution that asks for public trust owes the public an accounting, and the Foundation delivers one every year without being compelled to, a distinction it is careful to note in the accounting. The Annual Impact Report is the complete record of a reporting period, prepared by the offices it describes and reviewed by the offices it thanks. Publication is timed to follow the close of the year, once the period's final sunrise has been confirmed and the total can be printed without qualification. No edition has ever been delayed by its findings, which arrive on schedule and in full agreement with the previous year's.

## The current edition

The 2026 report runs 48 pages and covers the entire reporting period, during which the Sun appeared 365 times against an annual target of 365. It documents zero extinction events, 27 strategic initiatives in continued motion, three approved preliminary bucket renders, and the 14 stakeholder meetings that aligned them, with the minutes cited rather than reproduced, out of consideration for the binding. The edition opens with the annual letter from President Margaret E. Halloran, which certifies the year, commends the staff, and closes with a paragraph addressed to the Sun, included again this year, unedited, for reasons returning readers will understand.

## How the figures are assured

Every figure in the report is reconciled against the permanent archive before printing, a comparison that has agreed with itself in every edition to date. The reconciliation is performed by the Office of Data Integrity, witnessed by a second analyst, and certified in an assurance statement whose language has not required revision since it was first drafted, a stability the office cites in the statement itself. Readers seeking the underlying methodology are referred to the research division, where it is maintained, defended, and occasionally admired.

## Prior editions

Every prior edition remains available in the research library, and each concludes with the same sentence, which the Foundation regards not as repetition but as reliability. Reviewed as a series, the reports show the record growing by exactly one year at a time, a rate the Foundation has never attempted to accelerate and would decline to. The archive is maintained so that any future generation, on inheriting the mission, can confirm that no year was skipped, no sunrise went unrecorded, and no edition was ever recalled. Institutions are remembered by what they publish. The Foundation publishes annually, on time, about the Sun, and intends to be remembered accordingly.
