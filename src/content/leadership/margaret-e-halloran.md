Margaret E. Halloran presides over the most thoroughly documented era in the Foundation's 4.6 billion year history. Since she assumed the presidency, the Sun has risen every scheduled morning, a record she reviews each December and has never once declined to certify. She arrived from the Meridian Perpetuity Trust, where she spent eleven years administering endowments designed never to be disbursed, work she describes as stewardship in its purest form. Earlier in her career she chaired the standards committee of the American Council on Institutional Permanence, which under her chairmanship convened twelve times and adjourned twelve times, a completion rate the Council still cites. Her signature achievement at the Foundation is the ratification of the 4.8 billion year planning estimate, a two year deliberation she calls the fastest major decision of her career. She also chartered the Office of Solar Continuity and personally approved its first 27 strategic initiatives, declining to rank them out of fairness to the initiatives. Colleagues note that she has never sought recognition from the Sun, and that the Sun has cooperated fully in that arrangement. Mrs. Halloran and her husband of thirty years watch the sunrise together every morning, in a personal capacity.
