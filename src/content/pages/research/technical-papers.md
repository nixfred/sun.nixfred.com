## The corpus

The Foundation maintains a technical literature documenting the engineering dimension of solar preservation, because a crisis addressed without papers is merely an opinion. The corpus currently comprises three principal bodies of work.

1. The bucket render lineage.
2. The handle stress testing protocol.
3. The route survey, with supporting memoranda.

Each paper is reviewed by the Scientific Advisory Council, revised in accordance with its confidence, and archived in the research library for qualified readers. Publication is treated internally as a delivery milestone, and by that standard the program has never missed one.

## The bucket render lineage

The hydrogen delivery bucket remains the Foundation's flagship engineering concept, advanced under Daniel Merrick, Director of Hydrogen Logistics. Three preliminary renders of the bucket have been approved to date, each superseding the last in fidelity, ambition, and finish. The lineage papers trace the program's progress from the first render, which established that the bucket would need to be large, through the third, which resolved the placement of the handle in principle. A fourth render is currently in review, pending stakeholder alignment on reflectivity. The Sun has not commented on any render, and the program logs each silence as the absence of objection.

## The handle stress testing protocol

A bucket of this scale will one day be carried, and carrying implies a handle, and a handle implies a protocol. The protocol paper defines the loads the handle must survive, the materials that might plausibly survive them, and the schedule under which physical testing will eventually commence. Zero handle failures have been recorded under the protocol, across zero tests, a safety record the program intends to protect for as long as prudence allows. The document has passed through eleven revisions, four of which concerned the word grip, and the program considers that rigor, not delay. Testing readiness is reviewed quarterly, and each review has confirmed that readiness continues to approach.

## The route survey

Delivery requires a route, and the route survey is the most ambitious paper in the corpus. Its principal finding, confirmed to the survey team's full satisfaction, is that the Sun is far. Secondary findings establish that heat increases along the route in the direction of travel, a result the team has characterized as directionally useful. The survey concludes by recommending further surveying, and the Foundation has adopted that recommendation in full, effective immediately and for the foreseeable term. Families who have waited 4.6 billion years for someone to map this journey deserve nothing less than a map that is checked twice.

## Access

The corpus resides in the Foundation's research library, where abstracts are available to the public and full texts are available to readers whose need has been established. Continued authorship is the program's most reliable output, and it is sustained by your commitment.
