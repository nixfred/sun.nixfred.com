## 1. Lead Story: THE SUN ROSE AGAIN

The Sun rose again this morning, a development that will be familiar to readers of Issue 47. The confirmation desk logged the appearance at first light and noted no deviation in position, schedule, or attitude. This is the second consecutive issue to lead with a confirmed sunrise, a streak the editors now track separately from the sunrise streak itself, because records deserve records. The Sun offered no statement, extending a media silence of approximately 4.6 billion years, and the Office of Public Awareness has advised readers to interpret the silence as continuity rather than comment, there being no evidence for comment.

## 3. Field Note: Community Dawn Event

A correspondent attended the Community Dawn Event held outside Chattanooga, where 41 residents gathered before first light, which is the only hour at which the event can begin honestly. Coffee was served, and the horizon was monitored by all present. At the scheduled moment the Sun rose into position, and the attendees applauded. The applause was not acknowledged, which attendees had been briefed to expect. All 41 participants personally observed the Sun, an observation rate of 100 percent that the Awareness office described as consistent with national figures. Vice President Gerald Fontaine, present in an official capacity, remarked that "when people show up, the Sun does too." General Counsel has since reviewed the remark and found it aspirational rather than causal, and both offices consider the matter closed, which for this Foundation constitutes a warm ending.
