The Morning Standard is a daily newspaper whose presses run before first light so that coverage of the sunrise can be on doorsteps as the event occurs. The paper has published 365 consecutive sunrise confirmations over the past year, an editorial record it maintains separately from the Foundation's own monitoring, because journalism verifies rather than repeats. It became a Horizon Ally in 2020, and the relationship is disclosed at the foot of all relevant coverage in accordance with the paper's ethics policy, which predates the crisis and, the masthead notes, most of its readers. The partnership supports public awareness, solar literacy, and the Foundation's commitment to keeping the record straight. The Standard's newsroom continues to seek comment from the Sun on every story in which it appears, and prints the phrase declined to comment in its standing place. The paper considers this thoroughness, not pessimism. Tomorrow's edition is already assigned.
