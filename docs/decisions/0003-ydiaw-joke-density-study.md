# Decision 0003: The YDIAW density study

Date: 2026-07-25
Status: Accepted as the copy engine specification.

Fred's instruction: study yourdoingitallwrong.com, which lands roughly a
joke per sentence without repeating itself, and match that level. Source
studied: the live site's homepage, FAQ, testimonials, calculator, contact
block, footer, and the wife economics blog post, read from the local repo.

## The mechanics found

1. One premise, rotated through every institutional surface. YDIAW asserts
   "you are wrong" through pricing, guarantees, testimonials, the street
   address, the phone line, the email response time, GPS directions, the
   coupon code, and the footer. The premise never changes. The delivery
   surface never repeats.

2. A permanent background joke that costs zero words. The site about
   wrongness misuses your and you're in its own name and in nearly every
   sentence, and never acknowledges it. It is the crown jewel: a joke that
   fires on every line without being told.

3. Specificity as the delivery vehicle. 2.7 million unique ways to be
   wrong. 3 to 5 business days of emotional recovery. Wrongness dividends
   paid out during arguments in 2047. Exact numbers make absurd claims
   institutional.

4. Self defeating institutional honesty. We do not fix problems, we charge
   you to know about them. Guarantees that guarantee failure. Premium
   neglect as a service tier.

5. Inverted trust marks. ISO 9001 Non-Compliant. BBB Rating F+. All Wrongs
   Reserved. Coupon code NODISCOUNTS. The genre's credibility furniture,
   sabotaged one piece at a time.

6. The reader is complicit and attacked. Find this funny? WRONG.

Every sentence must do two jobs: read as credible institutional prose in
isolation, and carry a payload when parsed. Rotate mechanisms so no two
consecutive sentences use the same one. When a sentence cannot carry a
payload without breaking register, it earns its place by setting up the
next one. Shared universe note: YDIAW is a Division of Wrong Holdings LLC.
The Foundation is a Wholly Concerned Subsidiary of the same. Keep that
thread.
