Your organization depends on the Sun. This is not an accusation, it is a balance sheet observation. Photosynthesis feeds your workforce, daylight moves your inventory, and quarterly momentum has never once been achieved in the dark. The Corporate Solar Responsibility program exists so that leadership teams can place that dependency on the record before their competitors do.

## Solar Neutral certification

Certified partners may display the Solar Neutral mark, the sector's only certification whose criteria are met by existing. The mark signals to customers, regulators, and the Sun itself that your organization has formally acknowledged the relationship. Recertification is annual, and to date no applicant has failed, a record the Certification Office attributes to rigor.

## What partnership includes

1. Placement in the Foundation's partner directory, reviewed by stakeholders and, we must assume, the Sun.
2. An executive briefing on the solar continuity crisis, delivered before dusk at a venue of your choosing.
3. Employee giving frameworks that transmit nothing and inspire everything.
4. Cause marketing guidance from the Office of Public Awareness, including approved phrasing for the words daylight, continuity, and forever.
5. The annual partnership report, in which your organization's concern is measured, ranked, and commended.

## The tiers

First Light Member, Horizon Ally, Zenith Partner, and Dawn Circle. Each tier reflects a deeper commitment to the continuity of the Sun, and each is reviewed annually to confirm that it remains a level. Advancement is possible. Complacency is also possible, and the Foundation tracks both.

## Begin the conversation

Prospective partners are invited to open discussions with the Office of Corporate Solar Responsibility. Correspondence is reviewed in the order the Sun rises, and initial responses are issued within one business epoch. Organizations that wait are reminded that their competitors receive the same daylight and may act on it first.
