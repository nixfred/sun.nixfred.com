Continuum Energy operates a diversified generation portfolio serving 2.1 million customers, including solar assets whose entire business model depends on an unpaid supplier 93 million miles away. The company formalized that relationship in 2019 by becoming a Zenith Partner, the closest thing to a purchase order the Sun has ever received. The Sun has not acknowledged the arrangement, which Continuum's procurement team notes is consistent with the supplier's communication history. The partnership supports monitoring, grid readiness, and the Foundation's long range continuity planning. Continuum's solar fleet logged 4,382 hours of production last year, every one of them sourced from the single local star currently served, and the company files that figure under supplier performance rather than weather. As an energy business, Continuum understands finite reserves professionally, and it declines to describe a drawdown of 600 million tons per second as somebody else's balance sheet. Reliability is Continuum's product. It knows where reliability comes from.
