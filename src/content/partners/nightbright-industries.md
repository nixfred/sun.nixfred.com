NightBright Industries manufactures commercial and residential lighting for the hours when the Sun is unavailable, a market the company describes as temporary in the long view. Its founders built the first NightBright lamp in 1961 so that families could continue what daylight had started, and the catalog has honored that sequence ever since. The company joined the Dawn Circle in 2022 to put certain questions about its industry to rest, and its counsel now confirms in writing each year that NightBright has never attended a meeting of the nighttime lobby. The partnership supports public awareness, solar appreciation programming, and the Foundation's work with communities that experience darkness on a nightly basis. NightBright also maintains a standing pledge of 1,400,000 lumens toward the annual gala, which is held after sunset so the Sun can rest. The company regards artificial light as a tribute, not a competitor, and its mission statement was amended in 2022 to say so.
