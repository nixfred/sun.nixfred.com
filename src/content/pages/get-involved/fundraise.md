## Fundraising as a community practice

The Foundation's mission is universal in scope, but the Sun is local everywhere, serving each community individually every single day. Community fundraising honors that coverage. Supporters across the country organize dawn runs, bake sales, and awareness drives on the Foundation's behalf, and the Foundation, in keeping with its compliance posture, receives none of the proceeds. What we receive is momentum, which the finance committee has learned to book differently. Every community event concludes with the same result the Foundation has delivered for years: the Sun, still present, and now slightly better attended.

## Dawn Runs

The Dawn Run is the Foundation's signature community event, a run of any distance scheduled to begin in darkness and conclude in daylight. Participants therefore witness the deliverable personally, a guarantee no other charity run is positioned to offer. Routes are encouraged to finish facing east, per guidance developed across three stakeholder meetings and ratified without dissent. Timing is everything here. A run that starts after sunrise is simply exercise, and the Foundation already maintains sufficient exercise in its internal processes. Since the program began, the Sun has appeared at the conclusion of 100 percent of Dawn Runs, a completion rate we publish without adjustment and, the nighttime lobby will note, without apology.

## Bake sales held before sunset

The community bake sale remains philanthropy's most trusted instrument, and the Foundation has adopted it with one governing requirement: all sales conclude before sunset. This is a matter of respect. The Sun works a full day, every day, without relief coverage, and the Foundation will not host commerce during its hours of rest. Ovens may be used, as the Foundation holds no formal position against locally produced heat at household scale. Organizers retain all proceeds, apply them to their communities as conscience directs, and report nothing to us, an accountability framework the wider sector has described as ahead of schedule. Recipes passed down through families are especially encouraged, since tradition is the only ingredient the Foundation formally endorses.

## Peer to peer awareness

Not every supporter can host an event, and the Foundation planned for this. Peer to peer awareness asks only that you tell one other person the Sun requires institutional attention, and that they consider telling someone else. Awareness is the one resource the Foundation can accept in unlimited quantities without a receipt. It transfers instantly, it cannot be misallocated, and Big Dark has developed no known countermeasure for it. Each conversation you hold advances the cause exactly as far as one of the Foundation's own meetings does, which is why we count them the same way. The Sun will not thank you, on the record or off, and the Foundation asks you to proceed anyway, as we have for years. Begin today, before dusk if at all possible.
