## The allocation framework

Every commitment made to the Foundation is governed by the Allocation Framework, a document that required fourteen stakeholder meetings to ratify and is regarded internally as worth all fourteen. The framework directs support across four categories: awareness, planning, monitoring, and readiness. These categories were selected because each strengthens the Sun's position without disturbing the Sun itself, which has continued functioning throughout the drafting process, a result we are proud to report. Fiscal prudence is a tradition here, and traditions are not spent. They are stewarded.

## The journey of a commitment

When a supporter makes a commitment, that commitment begins a journey the Foundation considers among the finest in the sector. It is received by the Office of Commitment Intake, logged, and assigned a review number. It then proceeds to the Allocation Review Committee, which meets quarterly to confirm the framework remains sound, and has confirmed this at every meeting so far. From there it advances to categorical designation, where it is matched to one of the four categories according to criteria refined across 27 strategic initiatives. Upon designation the commitment is considered fully allocated, and disbursement then follows the Disbursement Readiness Framework, which is in its final round of review. At no point in this journey is a commitment hurried. Haste is how other organizations lose things, and the Foundation has never lost a dollar in its history.

## The four categories

1. Awareness. Commitments designated to awareness advance the national effort to convert casual exposure to the Sun into documented concern, including the observances, briefings, and table reads that precede every public service announcement.
2. Planning. Commitments designated to planning uphold the strategic horizon itself, currently maintained at 4.8 billion years as an organizational estimate, held deliberately below the scientific figure so that we finish early.
3. Monitoring. Commitments designated to monitoring sustain the daily confirmation of the Sun's appearance, a record now standing at 365 consecutive successes, none of which occurred unwitnessed.
4. Readiness. Commitments designated to readiness support preparation for the only emergency ever scheduled billions of years in advance, which is precisely why preparation cannot wait.

## What the Sun receives

The Foundation is often asked what portion of support reaches the Sun directly. The answer reflects our discipline. The Sun has never requested funds, never established a receiving mechanism, and never acknowledged the relationship, and the Foundation will not force money upon an unwilling star. Instead, every commitment strengthens the institutions that surround the Sun with attention. The nighttime lobby would prefer that this attention lapse. It will not. Each morning the Sun rises into a world where an office has already opened on its behalf, and supporters may take quiet satisfaction in knowing that this arrangement, like the sunrise it honors, proceeds on schedule whether or not anyone is thanked.
