# Design System

## Overall visual direction

Major global nonprofit.
Editorial and institutional.
Solar imagery without science fiction excess.
Modern but not trendy.
Confident use of whitespace.
Dense enough to reward exploration.

## Palette

Solar Gold:
Primary action and highlights.

Deep Space Navy:
Primary backgrounds and institutional seriousness.

Off White:
Editorial pages.

Warm Gray:
Secondary backgrounds.

Warning Red:
Crisis alerts only.

Do not make the entire site yellow and black.
Do not mimic a solar energy installer.
Do not make it look like a NASA fan site.

## Typography

Use a strong editorial serif for major headlines.

Use a neutral modern sans serif for body, navigation, data, and controls.

Use uppercase labels sparingly.

Use large scale typography for campaign statements.

## Logo direction

Primary mark:
A formal circular solar seal with a partially depleted inner ring.

Possible visual structure:

Outer ring:
SAVE THE SUN FOUNDATION

Center:
Minimal solar disk with one missing segment.

Established line:
EST. 4,600,000,000 BC

The logo must look credible at small and large sizes.

## Graphic system

Solar disk diagrams.

Timeline graphics.

Hydrogen depletion charts.

Impact dashboards.

Policy maps.

Program icons.

Annual report covers.

Sunspot adoption certificates.

Corporate partnership badges.

Solar Neutral certification mark.

## Motion

Slow solar surface movement.

Subtle number count ups.

Status pulse.

Countdown seconds.

Scroll triggered chart reveals.

No comedic bouncing.
No cartoon explosions.
No excessive parallax.

## Photography and imagery

Use:

Solar observatory imagery.

Atmospheric light.

Human faces looking toward daylight.

Institutional meetings.

Classrooms.

Corporate leaders.

Research environments.

Generational family imagery.

Avoid:

Memes.

Sunglasses on the Sun.

Cartoon planets.

Obvious stock photo handshakes unless deliberately used as institutional texture.

## Accessibility

Strong contrast.

Visible focus states.

Keyboard navigation.

Reduced motion mode.

Semantic headings.

Accessible accordions and dialogs.

Alt text.

No essential information conveyed only through color.
