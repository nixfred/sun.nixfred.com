President Margaret E. Halloran is available for interviews before dusk. Photographers are reminded that the Sun does not sign releases, has not acknowledged the relationship, and appears on schedule anyway, an arrangement General Counsel Charles W. Abernathy has reviewed and elected not to disturb.
