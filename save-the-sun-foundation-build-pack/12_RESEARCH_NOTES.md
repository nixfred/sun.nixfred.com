## Science baseline

Use the approximate phrase "about five billion years" in science heavy sections.

The campaign may use "4.8 billion years" as its branded countdown estimate, but should label it as an organizational planning estimate rather than claiming impossible precision.

The Sun is approximately 4.6 billion years old.

It is roughly midway through its main sequence lifetime.

It fuses hydrogen into helium.

It will eventually enter a red giant phase.

## Satirical opportunity

Real nonprofits strengthen credibility through transparency, impact reporting, corporate partnerships, symbolic giving, donor journeys, and recurring contributions.

The Foundation should reproduce these structures faithfully, then fill them with dead serious solar continuity language.

## Important distinction

The site should parody institutional behavior, not people who support real charities.

The target is:

Performative urgency.

Administrative self preservation.

Vague impact.

Corporate virtue signaling.

Long term strategic planning.

Taking credit for unrelated outcomes.

Fear based fundraising.

Organizational expansion.
